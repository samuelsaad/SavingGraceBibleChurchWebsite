# Related themes

Mode: Read for the sermon section; Operate for the private evaluator. This is a
narrow extension of the incumbent Astra shell and scoped Claude sermon design.
The delivery and human-release boundary is `related-themes-delivery-plan.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Extend sermon discovery with description-based thematic connections,
distinct from the existing metadata-based Related sermons.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Astra's shared shell and Claude's serif sermon titles,
neutral reading ground, gold rules, book tabs and compact related entries.
The evaluator uses mineral-blue text, pale-sky panels and native form controls.

STORY: Read the sermon, explore existing metadata relationships, then inspect
provisional thematic connections. Assigned reviewers independently read complete
anonymous descriptions, rate candidates, compare blinded lists and download
private ratings.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The sermon opening remains intact. At the new section, a normal
section heading and evaluation notice precede familiar related entries. The
evaluator opens with its purpose and privacy instructions, then the first anchor;
its download action follows all ratings and the independence confirmation.

FORM: Direct, code-led extension of existing components; no new visual-world
selection or generated comp. Candidate links and return links connect each list
comparison to its rating fieldset. Native fragment navigation needs no animation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Completed disposition

The independent agent finish-review handoff is **ship within protected evaluation
scope**. Its P2
candidate-navigation finding is resolved by keyboard-focusable candidate and
comparison destinations, native links, and explicit return links. The detector
reported zero primary findings. The remaining
1.5rem and 1.75rem evaluator heading-size detector advisories are intentional
local hierarchy choices, not additions to the global token scale.

The documentation pass inspected complete anonymous Related themes captures at
1440, 390 and 320 pixels, supplementing the earlier context captures. Evidence,
incumbent comparison and limitations are in `docs/design/related-themes.md`.
`PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` are preserved. No shipping
raster is introduced by this extension.

## Content and access invariants

Description-based suggestions have their own section and contents-rail link.
Metadata relationship reasons remain exclusive to Related sermons. Neither
section presents numeric similarity scores. Normal visitor Related themes stays
disabled until genuine independent blinded human calibration, policy lock and
positive untouched-holdout evidence satisfy the delivery gate.

The evaluator hides titles, identities, method labels and scores; it renders
complete descriptions and labels candidates only within each anchor. Ratings
download locally, with no submission from the review page. Screenshots use only
fictional anonymous fixtures in ignored `private/related-themes/screenshots/`.
This design record is not human usefulness evidence or publication approval.
