# Saving Grace Bible Church

## People and purpose

This is the visitor website for Saving Grace Bible Church in Westmeadows, Melbourne. It serves people exploring Christianity, newcomers planning a first visit, people seeking a church home, and members finding teaching and church information. The first task is to understand the church and plan a Sunday visit; the continuing task is to discover its sermons, beliefs, ministries and events.

## Confirmed truth

The tracked church content describes Christ-centred worship, expository preaching and a diverse congregation united through the Gospel. Sunday morning service is 10:30 am–12:30 pm; the informal evening service is 5:30–7 pm. The address is Unit 5/217–219 Mickleham Road, Westmeadows VIC 3049. Retain all doctrinal wording, dates, leaders, ministry details and contact information from the existing content registry, without adding claims. Children’s activities are described for the evening, not assumed for the morning.
